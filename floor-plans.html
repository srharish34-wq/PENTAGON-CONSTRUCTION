How it works

Your Home Construction Journey

From understanding your requirements to breaking ground, every stage is planned and explained before it happens.

01

Tell Us Your Requirements

Land dimensions, family requirements, number of floors, rooms, style, budget and future needs.

02

Site Assessment

Understand the plot, access, orientation, site conditions and construction requirements.

03

Concept & Floor Plan

Layout, elevation concept and material palette drafted for your review and approval.

04

Structural Design & Estimate

Structural drawings prepared alongside a detailed construction estimate for your home.

05

Working Drawings

Electrical, plumbing and finishing drawings issued so the site team can execute with precision.

06

Approvals & Documentation

Assistance with local body approvals and documentation wherever required.

07

Construction Agreement

Scope, specifications, price and project terms are finalised.

08

Construction Begins

With the design, estimate and agreement finalised, your home moves from planning to construction.
