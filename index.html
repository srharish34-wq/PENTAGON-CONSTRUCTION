How we work

From first site visit to handover

Six stages, each with a signed-off deliverable before the next one starts. You always know which stage your project is in.

  1. 1

    Site visit & requirement study

    We walk your plot, check soil, access, setbacks and existing services, then talk through what you actually want to build.

  2. 2

    Drawings & itemised estimate

    Architectural and structural drawings with a line-by-line BOQ. No lump-sum numbers you can't check.

  3. 3

    Approvals & documentation

    We prepare and file the drawings for municipal and government clearances, and follow them up until sanction.

  4. 4

    Foundation & structure

    Excavation, footing, RCC framing and slab work, supervised by a qualified engineer with material tests on record.

  5. 5

    Finishing & services

    Plastering, flooring, electrical, plumbing, painting and interiors, with brand choices confirmed by you in writing.

  6. 6

    Snagging & handover

    Joint inspection, snag list closed out, then keys, as-built drawings and warranty documents handed over.
