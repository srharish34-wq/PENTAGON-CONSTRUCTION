Ask us directly

Pentagan Engineers & Constructions, formerly Pentagon Engineers and Associates, was founded in April 2011. We are a civil engineering solutions company covering residential buildings, high-rise structures, villas, industrial facilities, factories, workshops, roads and pavement works.

The company is led by Er. N. Prakash Chetty, Founder & CEO, a qualified civil engineer (B.Tech Civil, MBA) with 20+ years of hands-on construction experience.

Our portfolio includes independent houses and villas, apartments, corporate and commercial interiors, showrooms, compound walls and external development, industrial and factory buildings, and government office works.

Our clients include L&W Constructions, CASA GRAND, Innovative Engineers, Indira Projects, Baashyam Constructions, VGN Projects, Hanu Reddy Reality, Capgemini and government bodies such as the Tamil Nadu Rural Transformation Project.

We work with a team of technical staff and administrators supported by a skilled and unskilled workforce of 150+, so projects can be executed with our own resources.

Our office is at 158/10B, Ambal Nagar Main Road, Sennerkuppam, Poonamallee, Chennai - 56. Most of our projects are across Chennai and surrounding locations, and we also take up larger projects outside the city.
